Key生产者消费者用途

stream:profile:draft-generated画像推理push-gateway触发 SSE
stream:reply:suggestion-generated回复推理push-gateway触发 SSE
stream:tag:recommend-generated标签推理push-gateway触发 SSE
stream:schedule:task-suggested日程解析push-gateway待办建议
stream:adoption:recorded各业务服务stat / online-learning采纳回写
stream:customer:owner-changedadminprofile / schedule离职交接联动

⛔ 架构红线（FR-2.C1~C4，已写入合同）

  • 🔴 C1：禁止调用企业微信「代发消息」API
  • 🔴 C2：AI 输出仅渲染为建议卡片，发送动作由顾问在企微原生窗口手动完成
  • 🔴 C3：UI 对 AI 内容永久标注「AI 建议」
  • 🔴 C4：PR checklist + 代码扫描强制拦截代发相关代码
